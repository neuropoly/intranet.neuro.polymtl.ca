# <span>🔑</span> LinkedIn administration

Roles and settings of the NeuroPoly LinkedIn page and the SCT showcase page. For how to get access, switch identity, repost and write posts, see the [LinkedIn guidelines](linkedin.md).

## Roles

| Role | Who | Can do |
| ---- | --- | ------ |
| Super admin | PIs and the page managers (currently Paul Hoareau and Youssef Laatar) | Everything, including managing other admins and page settings |
| Content admin | All other students | Post, repost, react and comment as the page |

These are LinkedIn's own role names, see [LinkedIn Page admin roles](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a541981). Roles are given separately on the NeuroPoly page and on the SCT showcase page.

```{note}
Access is given through roles on your personal account. Never share a LinkedIn password.
```

## Collaborative post invitations

When the NeuroPoly or SCT page is invited as a collaborator on a post, an admin has to accept the invitation for the page to be listed on it (see [collaborative posts](linkedin.md#collaborative-posts)).

## Settings and analytics

- Super admins manage page details (description, logo, banner, links) and roles.
- Page analytics (followers, post impressions) are visible to admins. Checking them from time to time shows which posts work well.
