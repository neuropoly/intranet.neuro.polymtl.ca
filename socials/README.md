## Rules for every channel

```{important}
- **Consent first.** Get the person's OK before posting about them, and for any photo showing them.
- **No unpublished results** without approval from the supervisor and all co-authors.
- **No confidential or participant data.** No images or details that could identify study participants.
- **Check spellings** of names, affiliations and titles against the person's own profile.
```
