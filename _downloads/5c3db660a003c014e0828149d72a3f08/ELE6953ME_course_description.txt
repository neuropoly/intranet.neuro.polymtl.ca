              ELE6953ME – Electromagnetic Compatibility: Theory and Practice
                                   Polytechnique Montréal, Winter 2027

Course description
Fundamental principles of Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC); electromagnetic noise and interference;
coupling mechanisms; mitigation and filtering techniques; EMC-oriented design principles for electrical and
electronic systems; regulatory framework and industrial EMC standards; emission and immunity requirements;
electromagnetic interactions involving cables; crosstalk phenomena; capacitive, inductive, and radiated
coupling; grounding and shielding practices for equipment and digital circuits; power integrity and Power
Distribution Network (PDN) design; radiation mechanisms and common-mode noise conversion; conducted
and radiated emissions; EMC measurement techniques; practical mitigation and troubleshooting methods;
ESD, EFT/Burst, surge immunity; conducted and radiated RF immunity; three-hour industrial visit to
an EMC/RF testing laboratory; EMC instrumentation and test setups; near-field probing and debugging
techniques; interpretation of EMC standards, compliance requirements, and industrial test reports.

Syllabus

Introduction to electromagnetic compatibility                                                            3h
Fundamentals and classification of electromagnetic noise and interference. Mitigation and filtering tech-
niques. EMC design principles and practical measures for designing for compliance. Regulatory framework,
applicable standards, and emission and immunity limits across standards and industries.

Cabling                                                                                                  3h
Interaction of electromagnetic fields with cables. Crosstalk between adjacent cables. Principles and behavior
of shielded and unshielded cables. Capacitive (electric), inductive (magnetic), and electromagnetic coupling.

Grounding                                                                                                3h
AC power distribution and protective grounding. Signal ground references, equipment grounding, digital
circuit grounding, and ground loops.

Noise and filtering techniques                                                                           6h
Electromagnetic noise fundamentals and coupling mechanisms. Frequency-domain analysis, passive filters,
and common-mode filtering. Power integrity, printed circuit board (PCB) design and routing, measurement,
and debugging.

Syllabus (continued)

Shielding                                                                                               3h
Shielding effectiveness fundamentals, material selection, and material properties. Cable shielding and
grounding strategies. Shielding design for enclosures and PCBs. Measurement and validation of shielding
performance.

Digital power distribution and radiation mechanisms                                                     6h
Power distribution network (PDN) fundamentals. Switching noise and simultaneous switching noise (SSN).
Decoupling strategies and capacitor optimization. PCB layer stack-up and return-current control. Power-plane
resonances and cavity radiation. Common-mode noise conversion, radiation mechanisms, and PDN-level
EMI mitigation.

Conducted emissions                                                                                     3h
Coupling paths and propagation. Line impedance stabilization networks (LISNs) and measurement principles.
Applicable EMC standards and limits. Effects of grounding and bonding. Practical troubleshooting.

Radiated emissions                                                                                      3h
Radiation mechanisms and sources at PCB and system levels. Measurement techniques and test setups. EMC
standards and compliance requirements. Design techniques for reducing emissions.

RF and transient immunity                                                                               6h
Immunity fundamentals. Radiated RF immunity and field-to-system coupling. Conducted RF immunity.
Electrical fast transients (EFT/burst) and switching disturbances. Surge and high-energy transient immunity,
electrostatic discharge (ESD), power supply variations, and low-frequency immunity. Design techniques for
improving immunity, measurement and test methods, and applicable standards.

Industrial visit to an EMC/RF testing laboratory                                                        3h
Hour 1 — Safety briefing and introduction to the laboratory facilities, semi-anechoic chamber, and key
measurement and test instruments.
Hour 2 — Conducted and radiated emissions demonstrations. Pre-compliance measurement, near-field
probing, and EMC debugging.
Hour 3 — Introduction to RF, ESD, and EFT immunity testing, demonstrations on actual equipment, and
discussion of applicable standards, test reports, and industry compliance issues.

Total                                                                                                  39 h
